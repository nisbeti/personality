First: The impact of basic beliefs on the formation of the psychology
1- Belief in Allah, Exalted is He, and His attributes
A person feels by nature that they are limited, deficient, and in need, and this feeling leads to a natural incapacity due to their inability to transcend their limitations, to complete their deficiencies, and to cover their needs through self-sufficiency. This generates a sense of helplessness, which is natural, meaning it is a consequence of human nature, and it is imposed on the individual, who cannot resist it or push it away. Rather, they attempt to alleviate this feeling by trying to secure for themselves everything that guarantees them freedom from need, such as wealth, food, clothing, and shelter. This gives rise to instinctive drives stemming from the survival instinct, such as greed, to acquire as much as possible of what they believe ensures their independence from need. They develop a desire to safeguard what they possess, leading to self-centeredness, where they prioritize themselves over others in all matters. Thus, various motivations arise within a person, pushing them to cover their deficiencies, needs, and helplessness, which are necessitated by the instinct of faith through the drives of survival. They remain in a state of constant anxiety and ongoing misery, striving diligently to satisfy their faith (helplessness, deficiency, need, and limitation) through the drives of the survival instinct.
The truth is that the manifestations of the instinct of faith are fulfilled through belief in Allah, Exalted is He, and belief in His attributes, meaning that the instinct of faith and its various manifestations are satisfied through correct concepts that fulfill faith, not through satisfying the survival instinct and its drives.
Allah, Exalted is He, says in Surah Taha: “So if there comes to you guidance from Me, then whoever follows My guidance will not go astray nor suffer, and whoever turns away from My remembrance, indeed, he will have a depressed life…” This noble verse indicates that whoever follows the guidance of Allah will not go astray or suffer, and following the guidance of Allah, Exalted is He, is contingent upon belief in Him. Thus, the guarantee of walking on a straight path and clear, evident steps results from following the guidance of Allah, and following His guidance prevents misery.
Belief in Allah, Exalted is He, fills the natural void within a person and results in a sanctification that satisfies human feelings of helplessness, deficiency, need, and limitation, and that He, Exalted is He, alone with His absolute attributes is the one who is needed for His ability over all things. There is tranquility in His sustaining power over this universe and His direct oversight, Exalted is He, over it, and in managing its affairs in the best order, with complete reassurance and trust in the management of this universe: “And among His signs is that the heaven and the earth stand by His command,” “He manages the affair,” “And who manages the affair? They will say Allah,” “Indeed, to Him belongs creation and command.”
