Linking Information to Reality:
A person is never free from the stages of learning they go through, and many people use the method of memorization and rote learning for the information they acquire. While this method may be suitable for a person up to a certain age and for specific subjects, it is not appropriate for them beyond that age, nor for all the things they learn. If they continue with the same method after reaching maturity, they will not become mature, because the essence of maturity is discernment. Therefore, some modern educational methods tend to adopt approaches that distance the learner from memorization and rote learning, such as experiential learning, play-based learning, self-directed learning, collaborative learning, and other methods that essentially link information to reality. This aims to equip the child or learner with this style of learning at an early stage. However, the question that must be posed here is: where is the reliance on the intellectual foundation in linking information to reality?
What is meant by linking information to reality is when learning occurs, and learning is not limited to sessions of knowledge and circles of study, but a person may hear information from a satellite channel, or hear it from someone, or read it in a pamphlet. The Islamic method in teaching requires taking information and ideas to act upon them, and this means two things:
1-     Linking information and ideas to reality
2-      The basis of this linkage is the intellectual foundation
This means forming concepts, and regarding psychology, this means the blending of motivation with the new concept, and forming the correct inclination that this concept necessitates.
