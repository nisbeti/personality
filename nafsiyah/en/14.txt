2- Artistic imagery: as His Exalted saying: “And do not backbite one another. Would one of you like to eat the flesh of his brother while he is dead? You would detest it.” In this imagery, there is an unmistakable aversion to the sin intended here, and the Qur'an, with such imagery, makes the legal ruling beloved to the Muslim or repels him from the disobedience of Allah, Exalted is He.
3- Stories: Allah, Exalted is He, said: "And thus We relate to you from the news of the towns what We strengthen thereby your heart," and He, Exalted is He, said: "Indeed, in their stories is a lesson for those of understanding." The Noble Qur'an employs the method of narrating the stories of the ancients, which contain lessons that contribute to the strengthening of the heart of the Messenger (peace and blessings be upon him) and the bearers of the message after him.
4- The Environment: Islam, in its rulings, legislations, and treatments, aims to create a sound environment that influences both the individual and the community towards the optimal shaping of the psychology. Thus, if an individual's piety falls short of elevating him or of deterring him from actions contrary to Islam, then public opinion serves as a deterrent for him. The bad appears in society like a black spot on the skin of a white bull, which stirs within the individual a sense of deficiency and criticism from others. Consequently, the surrounding environment assists the individual in shaping his psychology in the optimal and elevating manner, helping him avoid the pitfalls of the self. The Messenger of Allah (peace and blessings be upon him) said: "The believer is a mirror to his brother," so a Muslim sees through the eyes of his brother the faults and violations within himself, and he strives to correct and overcome them.
5- Encouragement and Warning: The Noble Qur'an employs the methods of encouragement and warning in most of its rulings, which stirs within the Muslim's soul a longing for what his heart desires and fear of what it does not desire. Indeed, He, Exalted is He, has encouraged the paradise and what it contains of everlasting bliss, and has warned against the fire and what it contains of painful torment. This makes the motivation for piety and adherence to the commands of Allah, Exalted is He, and avoidance of His prohibitions intrinsic. Thus, one refrains from unlawful earnings, awaiting what Allah has prepared for him in paradise, and abstains from unlawful gaze and forbidden sexual gratification, yearning for the houris in paradise. He draws closer to Allah, Exalted is He, fleeing from falling into sins and escaping from entering the fire. There are many examples in the Noble Qur'an and the pure Sunnah regarding encouragement and warning; I will mention here just one example.
