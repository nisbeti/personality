1- The concept of personality
The term personality is an artificial source, derived from the word (person), and it refers to any body that has height and appearance(1). Its usage has predominated for humans over others, without preventing its use and derivatives in non-human contexts, as it is applied to something that has prominence, manifestation, and distinction from its surroundings.
Philosophers have used it to denote the conscious self of its being, independent in its will. Perhaps in this usage, there is a transition from the material usage and the material description of a human in relation to its environment to the moral usage and moral description, in that what most distinguishes a human from its surroundings and from others is its mental and psychological reality. Although a human is distinguished in its creation, form, and body from other tangible creatures, Allah, Exalted is He, said: “Indeed, We have created man in the best stature”(2); yet its moral distinction, that is, its mental and psychological distinction from other tangible beings, is greater and more significant.
