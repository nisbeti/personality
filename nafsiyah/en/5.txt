Accordingly, the personality of every individual consists of two components, with no third: the intellectual and the psychological. The person's physical appearance, whether in terms of height or width, beauty or ugliness, or attire, whether harmonious or not, has no bearing on the judgment of a person's personality; rather, the judgment focuses on the manner of their thinking and the totality of their inclinations. The term personality is thus applied as an artificial source to the individual in terms of what distinguishes them, and indeed, the greatest distinction lies in their intellect and emotions.
2- The Concept of Mentality
The term "mentality" (al-aqliya) is an artificial source derived from the word "reason" (aql), and the word reason means thinking and perception. However, making the word an artificial source adds a new meaning in addition to the meaning of reason, which is the manner of reasoning, that is, how one comprehends things. This manner does not refer to the mechanism of thinking, as that is the meaning of reason, not mentality. What is observed in humans is that in their thinking and judgment about things, they start from a conceptual framework, and the conceptual framework is a general idea that allows for the construction of subsidiary ideas upon it. Therefore, it is called a framework, even though it is an idea; but due to its generality and comprehensiveness, it is suitable for building smaller ideas upon it. Thus, relying on a conceptual framework in the process of thinking serves as a basis for judging things, that is, a foundation for reasoning about them and issuing judgments on them; this is what is meant by mentality. Therefore, mentality refers to the manner of reasoning about things, that is, adopting a conceptual framework for judging things.
The Islamic personality takes Islamic belief as the foundation for thinking and judging things. If this personality encounters an incident, it views it as fate and divine decree, and thus acts according to the patience and accountability that this perspective entails, knowing that it is ultimately good for them. On the other hand, the capitalist personality views the incident as a calamity; as if it were the end of the world for them, due to the harm they have suffered as a result of that incident. However, if they emerge unscathed from the incident and gain some benefit from it, they begin to see it as good. Thus, their measure for judging a single matter is purely utilitarian, unlike the Islamic personality, which has made Islamic belief the basis for judging the incident and determining its stance towards it.
3- The Concept of Psychology
The term "psychology" is an artificial source, derived from the word "self" (nafs). Although the word "self" has different meanings in the Arabic language, the intended meaning here relates to a person's inclinations and emotional traits. They say: "So-and-so has a good self" if he loves good for others, and they say: "He has an elevated self" if he is humble. It is noted in usage the emotional aspects of being content with the truth, or feeling arrogance, or being distinguished by eagerness and greed, or other matters that are driven by emotions and motivations.
The formulation of the artificial source from this term necessitated an increase in meaning beyond the previous meaning of the self, in accordance with the linguistic rule "every increase in structure necessitates an increase in meaning."
