- Concepts: Concepts are the meanings of ideas, and the concept is the meaning of the idea, not the meaning of its words and sentences, meaning that the idea must have a reality that a person perceives and believes in. The perception of the reality of the idea can be either through sensory perception or through mental conception. If the reality of the idea is perceived and belief in its reality is established, the idea transforms into a concept in the mind of the one who has experienced that and believed it.
This can be expressed as follows:

Conceived by the mind

For example, in saying: honey is sweet, this statement is an idea, and since the senses perceive the sweetness of honey in reality and believe this perception, meaning they affirm the correspondence of this statement to the reality it refers to, then this statement and this idea transform into a concept in the mind of the one who perceives that and believes it.
