When a person makes belief the foundation for thinking, that is, a basis for linking reality with information, the formation of concepts will emerge from this intellectual foundation, resulting in the shaping of motivation and its blending with the concept, thus forming the appropriate inclination for that situation or behavior.

If a person proceeds in life with this method, that is, linking everything that their senses perceive from things, events, and relationships with the information they possess based on Islamic belief, that is, on the basis of the criteria that belief provides for this type of thoughts or judgments; the person gradually walks the path of forming their psychology, step by step, just as a great building is constructed brick by brick.

Some examples of this:

1- A man sees a woman, and a woman sees a man, which stimulates the instinct of reproduction in each of them. If he moves from sensation to action without passing through the stage of thought, he degrades to the level of an animal. However, if he reflects, he will link this reality with the information he has about it, building this connection on the basis of the intellectual principle he holds, specifically with the concept related to this reality. Accordingly, he determines his position based on the concept, whether to fulfill the desire or not. If his basis for linking and measuring is Islamic belief, he reaches the ruling of prohibiting the gaze if the seen is foreign to the viewer, thus refraining from looking. By repeatedly applying this mechanism every time this reality presents itself, the stimulation of desire becomes linked to the legal ruling, which emerges from his intellectual foundation, and inclinations that support this, accompanying that process, are formed, such as: fear of Allah, fear of His punishment, aversion to sin, and longing for Paradise... thus he refrains from sin out of hope for Allah's pleasure.

2- He sees wealth and sees things that require the money he does not possess. If his intellectual basis is not Islamic belief, he will likely feel regret for not being able to obtain it, and perhaps the instinctive drive will push him to engage in wrongful behavior to acquire money and achieve satisfaction. However, if his basis for measurement is Islamic belief, he links this reality to Allah's division of sustenance, thus finding reassurance in Allah's wisdom in testing him with that trial, and he remains patient, hoping for Allah's pleasure and what Allah has prepared for His servants in Paradise.

3- If Allah has not granted him sons; whenever he sees a reality related to sons, his position will be according to his intellectual basis. He will feel regret if his foundation is not Islamic belief, but he will be content, patient, and hopeful regarding what Allah has decreed for him, believing that Allah alone grants daughters and sons, and that He makes whom He wills barren.
