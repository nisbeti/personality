In other words, the Islamic creed has taken on the responsibility of shaping the human mind and psychology to create a distinguished personality, one that progresses in its thoughts, behaviors, and inclinations in an upward manner, ensuring the existence of that distinguished Islamic personality, safeguarding it, and guaranteeing its elevation and growth from good to better, and from high to higher.

This research will address the Islamic concepts that constitute the Islamic psychology, the concepts that ensure its preservation, and the concepts that guarantee its elevation. However, it will begin with the methods of creating the concept, that is, transforming intended ideas into concepts that influence one's judgment on things, affect one's motivations towards them, and impact the accompanying feelings of those motivations.

It has been stated in the research that an idea that affects the mentality and the psychology must transform into a concept, and it has been noted that the transformation of an idea into a concept can only occur through the sensory or conceptual understanding of the reality of the idea, along with the belief in this understanding of the reality of the idea. Sensory perception is related to tangible realities, while conceptual perception pertains to unseen realities that cannot be grasped by the senses.

The formation and construction of the psychology cannot occur all at once or in a single instance; rather, it must happen gradually, after the individual accepts the foundational creed upon which their mentality and psychology will be built. They begin to transform the ideas and information brought by the creed into concepts that act upon their judgments and inclinations, and this can be achieved in one of two ways:

1- Linking reality with information or ideas

2- Linking information and ideas with reality

The research will elaborate on each of these methods with detailed explanations and examples.

Linking reality with information:

A person lives in this universe with its:

- Things - Events - Relationships

They must determine their perspective towards what their senses perceive of those things, events, and relationships. They must take a stance on what occurs, regardless of their involvement in its occurrence, and they must engage in actions to satisfy their organic needs and instincts related to their surroundings. A stance must be accompanied by an inclination, in addition to the emergence of a specific motivation towards what occurs.

They will also perform actions and behaviors that correspond to what their senses perceive as a result of the stimulation of certain motivations that drive them to undertake those actions.

Naturally, for a human being, the sensation of reality generates thoughts about it, as a result of linking the perceived reality with the information they possess.

However, this is not sufficient; this linkage must align with their intellectual framework in order to achieve psychological and mental harmony; otherwise, their inclinations will contradict their concepts, leading to anxiety.
