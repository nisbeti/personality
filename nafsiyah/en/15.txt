6- The good example: Islam made it obligatory for the Muslim to take the Messenger of Allah, peace and blessings be upon him, as an example in all his deeds and words, so he strives to follow the Messenger’s action and word and holds to it, because obeying the Messenger in what he made obligatory saves the Muslim from Allah’s punishment and brings him closer to Allah the Exalted, and following him in the recommended acts is a means of nearness to Allah the Exalted. Allah the Exalted said: (You have in the Messenger of Allah an excellent example for whoever hopes for Allah and the Last Day and remembers Allah often), and He made following him a reason for Allah’s love for the servant. Allah the Exalted said: (Say: If you love Allah, then follow me, and Allah will love you and forgive you your sins) Al Imran/31.
7- Competition in good deeds: Islam urged Muslims to race in good deeds and to compete in drawing near to Allah the Exalted, and in this competition there is a polishing of the psychology and a rising with it. Allah the Exalted said: (For the like of this let the workers work), and He said: (And for that let the competitors compete) Al-Mutaffifin/26. And He said: (Race to forgiveness from your Lord and a Paradise as wide as the heavens and the earth, prepared for those who believe in Allah and His messengers) Al-Hadid/21, and He said: (So race to good deeds) Al-Baqarah/148 and Al-Ma’idah 48, and He said: (and among them is he who is foremost in good deeds) Fatir/32, and He said: (Those hasten to good deeds and they are foremost in them) Al-Mu’minun/61, and He said: (And the forerunners, the forerunners, those are the ones brought near) Al-Waqi‘ah/10.
Preserving and Developing the Psychology:
The formation of a sound Islamic psychology in a Muslim does not mean that it lasts and continues merely because it exists, since a human being is prone to forgetfulness, error, fluctuation and change, as the Prophet, peace and blessings be upon him, said: (The hearts of the servants are between two fingers of the Most Merciful; He turns them as He wills). For that reason the Islamic creed has sought to give its bearer means of protection through which he can preserve his psychological level and develop it, among them:
1- Instilling piety (taqwa) in the individual, which ensures that he keeps walking steadily with his drives and inclinations disciplined. Allah the Exalted said: (Indeed, those who are mindful of Allah, when an impulse from Satan touches them, remember, and at once they see clearly) Al-A‘raf/201.
