Separation of Thought from Feeling

Thought is what determines the type of feeling, this is true.

What is meant here by thought is the fundamental thought from which ideas arise that connect to feeling to transform it into inclination.

An idea does not transform feeling into inclination unless it becomes a concept, and the difference between an idea and a concept lies in the understanding of the meaning of the idea, the understanding of its reality, and the belief in this understanding. If this is achieved, the idea transforms into a concept in the mind of the one who understands its reality and believes it, and thus it becomes easier for it to connect with feeling, shaping and refining it and transforming it into inclination.

This is the essence.

However, the observable reality among some Muslims is that they are Muslims, but many of the fundamental Islamic ideas they hold have become disconnected from their feelings, and the reason???

The reason is that there are other concepts not derived from Islamic belief that have connected with their feelings.

Thus, a separation has occurred between their original thought and their feeling.

And in order for the previous statement to transform into a concept that understands its reality and believes it, I will explain this with an example:

The original state of a Muslim is that the concepts influencing their behavior and feelings should be derived from Islamic belief, and among these concepts is:

Provision is from Allah.

If the Muslim believes in its meaning, understands its reality, and affirms this understanding, it transforms into a concept for them. By recalling it when necessary, that is, when there is a reality related to it, it works to reshape the feelings associated with it, so the Muslim becomes:

- Assured of their provision and the provision of their children and those they are responsible for.

- Not fearing the interruption of provision, due to their firm belief that Allah is the Provider.

- Disconnecting in themselves the link between their work, effort, and striving and the provision that comes to them, so they perform their work within the limits of Allah's commands and prohibitions, without feeling any fear or anxiety about the impact of their adherence to Allah's limits on their provision, because the Provider is Allah, and the One who commands and prohibits is Allah.

- Remaining assured of the continuity of their provision, whether they see it as stable or changing, so they do not fear the future, and they despise ideas such as "save your white money for your black day" due to their firm belief that all days of the believer are white, and their tranquility regarding that.

- Whenever they see a reality related to provision, whether it concerns them or others, the concept strengthens within them, and the connection between the concept and inclination increases, allowing their feelings to reach the highest levels of psychological and intellectual elevation, moving from lower to higher.
