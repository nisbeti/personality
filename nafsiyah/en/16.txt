Allah, Exalted is He, has described the Noble Qur'an as a reminder for the pious in His saying: “And indeed, it is a reminder for the pious”. “Righteousness is not that you turn your faces toward the east and the west, but righteousness is in one who believes in Allah and the Last Day and the Angels and the Book and the Prophets and gives his wealth, in spite of love for it, to relatives, orphans, the needy, the traveler, those who ask [for help], and for freeing slaves; and establishes prayer and gives zakah; and those who fulfill their promise when they promise; and the patient in poverty and hardship and during battle. 2- The sound environment: Islam has imposed a specific framework on the Islamic community, highlighting the unity of thought, unity of feeling, and unity of system. Allah, Exalted is He, said: “But no, by your Lord, they will not believe until they make you judge in that which they dispute among themselves, and then find no discomfort in their souls from what you have judged and submit in [full, willing] submission” {Qur'an 4:65}. Thus, the judgment of Muslims by the Messenger (peace and blessings be upon him) is made a complement to faith, and faith is not complete without it. Allah, Exalted is He, also said: “Indeed, those who love to see scandal spread among those who have believed will have a painful punishment in this world and the Hereafter. And Allah knows, while you do not know” {Qur'an 24:19}. With this ruling, He ensured the direction of public opinion and the common custom according to the rulings of Allah, without allowing any harm to the honor of Muslims, making it a red line that cannot be crossed. Allah, Exalted is He, said: “If the hypocrites and those in whose hearts is disease and the rumor-mongers in the city do not desist, We will surely incite you against them” {Qur'an 33:60}. In this, there is a closure to the door of rumor-mongering and the spread of discouraging rumors among Muslims, which ensures the unity of feeling within the nation and guarantees the safety of the environment in which Muslims live.
Islam commands the Muslim to choose his close friend and companion wisely, and it has made piety (taqwa) the foundation of friendship. Allah, Exalted is He, said: {The close friends, some of them are enemies to others, except for the righteous}. And the Messenger of Allah (peace and blessings be upon him) said: "A person is upon the religion of his close friend, so let one of you look at whom he takes as a close friend."
3- Acquiring Islamic culture: Since the psychology is formed by linking information to reality, this connection contributes to shaping the psychology in a specific manner. Islam has encouraged the pursuit of knowledge and learning, and has elevated the status of scholars and the status of the student of knowledge. Allah, Exalted is He, said: “Allah will raise those who have believed among you and those who were given knowledge by degrees.” And He, Exalted is He, differentiated between the one who knows and the one who does not know, saying: “Say, ‘Are those who know equal to those who do not know?’”
4- Repentance and Seeking Forgiveness: Islam acknowledges human weakness, recognizing what is inherent in humans, such as forgetfulness and error. Thus, Exalted is He, overlooks what occurs from a person out of mistake or forgetfulness, in addition to acknowledging the possibility of sin from humans.
