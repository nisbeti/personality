There is a common type of fear that arises from the imbalance between the outcomes of taking action and the outcomes of not taking action, and both can cause harm. The error in this balance leads to fear of trivial matters and falling into dangers. For instance, the fear of an unjust ruler causing harm to an individual leads to harm to the entire community, and to himself as one of the community. Similarly, the fear of a soldier in the battlefield of death can lead to the annihilation of the entire army, of which he is a part. The fear of imprisonment for the sake of one's beliefs can lead to the loss of those beliefs, which is more painful than imprisonment or the loss of belief from existence in life. This fear is very dangerous for the community and leads to risks, and perhaps even to destruction and ruin.
However, fear can be beneficial and useful at times, and it must exist or be created, just as it can be harmful and destructive at other times, and it should not exist and must be treated and removed. Fear of real dangers is beneficial and obligatory, and being reckless about them and not fearing them is harmful and should not be the case, whether these dangers are to the individual himself or to his community. In this case, fear is the guardian and protector. Therefore, it is necessary to explain the dangers threatening the community so that they are taken into account and actions are taken to defend against them and remove these dangers. Fear of Allah and His punishment is beneficial and obligatory, and it is the guardian and protector. Thus, it is essential to instill fear of Allah in people's hearts and to explain the extent of Allah's punishment for committing sins and for disbelief, so that people may follow His religion, adhere to His commands, and avoid His prohibitions. This fear and similar fears are beneficial and useful, and they must exist and efforts should be made to instill them because they are the guardian and protector, ensuring that a person walks on the straight path.
Accordingly, fear is part of human nature, and concepts are what evoke it in humans and what distance it from them. It is one of the most dangerous matters for humans in some aspects, while also being one of the most beneficial in others. In order for a person to avoid its dangers and enjoy its benefits, he must govern it with true concepts alone, which are the concepts of Islam.
